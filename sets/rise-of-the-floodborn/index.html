Rise of the Floodborn: card list

Rise of the Floodborn is Disney Lorcana Set 2, released December 1, 2023. 204 cards first appeared in this set. Reprints of earlier cards are listed under the set they first appeared in.

159 Character, 27 Action, 18 Item. By rarity: 72 Common, 54 Uncommon, 48 Rare, 18 Super Rare, 12 Legendary. By ink: 34 Amber, 34 Amethyst, 34 Emerald, 34 Ruby, 34 Sapphire, 34 Steel.
