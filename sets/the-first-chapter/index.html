The First Chapter: card list

The First Chapter is Disney Lorcana Set 1, released September 1, 2023. 204 cards first appeared in this set. Reprints of earlier cards are listed under the set they first appeared in.

149 Character, 35 Action, 20 Item. By rarity: 72 Common, 54 Uncommon, 48 Rare, 18 Super Rare, 12 Legendary. By ink: 34 Amber, 34 Amethyst, 34 Emerald, 34 Ruby, 34 Sapphire, 34 Steel.
