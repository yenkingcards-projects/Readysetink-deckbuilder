Winterspell: card list

Winterspell is Disney Lorcana Set 11, released February 20, 2026. 200 cards first appeared in this set. Reprints of earlier cards are listed under the set they first appeared in.

151 Character, 24 Action, 18 Item, 7 Location. By rarity: 69 Common, 54 Uncommon, 47 Rare, 18 Super Rare, 12 Legendary. By ink: 34 Amber, 34 Ruby, 34 Steel, 33 Amethyst, 33 Emerald, 32 Sapphire.
