Into the Inklands: card list

Into the Inklands is Disney Lorcana Set 3, released March 8, 2024. 204 cards first appeared in this set. Reprints of earlier cards are listed under the set they first appeared in.

144 Character, 24 Action, 18 Item, 18 Location. By rarity: 72 Common, 54 Uncommon, 48 Rare, 18 Super Rare, 12 Legendary. By ink: 34 Amber, 34 Amethyst, 34 Emerald, 34 Ruby, 34 Sapphire, 34 Steel.
