Whispers in the Well: card list

Whispers in the Well is Disney Lorcana Set 10, released November 14, 2025. 202 cards first appeared in this set. Reprints of earlier cards are listed under the set they first appeared in.

149 Character, 26 Action, 18 Item, 9 Location. By rarity: 70 Common, 54 Uncommon, 48 Rare, 18 Super Rare, 12 Legendary. By ink: 34 Amber, 34 Amethyst, 34 Emerald, 34 Steel, 33 Ruby, 33 Sapphire.
