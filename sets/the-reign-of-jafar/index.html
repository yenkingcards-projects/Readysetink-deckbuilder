The Reign of Jafar: card list

The Reign of Jafar is Disney Lorcana Set 8, released June 6, 2025. 202 cards first appeared in this set. Reprints of earlier cards are listed under the set they first appeared in.

165 Character, 27 Action, 10 Item. By rarity: 70 Common, 54 Uncommon, 48 Rare, 18 Super Rare, 12 Legendary. By ink: 44 Amethyst, 44 Ruby, 44 Sapphire, 44 Emerald, 43 Amber, 43 Steel.
