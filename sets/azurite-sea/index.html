Azurite Sea: card list

Azurite Sea is Disney Lorcana Set 6, released November 25, 2024. 204 cards first appeared in this set. Reprints of earlier cards are listed under the set they first appeared in.

148 Character, 26 Action, 18 Item, 12 Location. By rarity: 72 Common, 54 Uncommon, 48 Rare, 18 Super Rare, 12 Legendary. By ink: 34 Amber, 34 Emerald, 34 Amethyst, 34 Ruby, 34 Sapphire, 34 Steel.
