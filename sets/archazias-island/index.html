Archazia's Island: card list

Archazia's Island is Disney Lorcana Set 7, released March 21, 2025. 205 cards first appeared in this set. Reprints of earlier cards are listed under the set they first appeared in.

174 Character, 17 Action, 14 Item. By rarity: 72 Common, 54 Uncommon, 48 Rare, 18 Super Rare, 12 Legendary, 1 Special. By ink: 45 Sapphire, 44 Amber, 44 Steel, 44 Emerald, 44 Ruby, 44 Amethyst.
