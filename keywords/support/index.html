Support (Disney Lorcana keyword)

Whenever this character quests, you may add its Strength to another chosen character's Strength for the rest of the turn.

Official rule 8.13 (Comprehensive Rules 2.2.0). This keyword appears on 58 cards: 58 Character. By ink: 31 Sapphire, 28 Amber, 3 Amethyst, 1 Steel, 1 Emerald.
