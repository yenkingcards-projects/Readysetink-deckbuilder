# Reckless: Disney Lorcana keyword

Source: https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/reckless/

The character can't quest, and you can't end your turn while it is ready and able to challenge. It can still be exerted to use abilities or sing songs. A strong but forced attacker. Official rule 8.7. 20 cards.
