Shift (Disney Lorcana keyword)

Pay the Shift cost instead of the ink cost to play this card on top of one of your characters with the same name. They become one stack. If the card underneath was exerted, the shifted character enters exerted.

In Lorcana: Some cards have Shift variants that relax the same-name requirement. Check the card text.

“Shift Elsa onto Elsa for four instead of paying full price.”

Official rule 8.10 (Comprehensive Rules 2.2.0). This keyword appears on 196 cards: 196 Character. By ink: 43 Steel, 42 Emerald, 40 Ruby, 37 Amethyst, 37 Amber, 36 Sapphire.
