## Official rulings

- Q: Can I use Luisa’s I Can Take It ability during the turn she’s played?
  A: Yes. The ability doesn’t have {E} as a cost, so it can be used the same turn she’s played.
- Q: If Lusia has 3 damage and I use her ability to move 1 damage to her, what happens?
  A: If there’s an opposing character you can choose to move the damage to, you’ll move 4 damage from her to chosen opposing character and Luisa won’t be banished. This means that she’ll temporarily have damage equal to her {W}, but because the effect is still resolving, the damage will be removed by the time a game state check occurs. If there's not an opposing character to move damage to after moving 1 damage to herself, then she has 4 damage when the game state check occurs and will be banished as a result.
