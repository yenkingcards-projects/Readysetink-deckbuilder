## Card text

> HARD WORK Whenever this character quests, put the top card of your deck facedown under him.
> A GIVING HEART When this character is banished in a challenge, you may put all cards that were under him under another chosen character or location of yours facedown.

## Official rulings

- Q: If Mickey Mouse is banished in a challenge, are the cards that were under him moved to another character or location faceup or facedown?
  A: Faceup. The triggered effect resolves after Mickey Mouse and all cards that were under him are put into the discard, where the facedown cards turn faceup.
