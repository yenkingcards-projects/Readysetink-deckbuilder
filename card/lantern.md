- Ink: Amber · Cost: 2 · Not inkable
- Set: The First Chapter #33 · Rarity: Rare
- Franchise: Tangled

## Card text

> BIRTHDAY LIGHTS ⟳ — You pay 1 ⬡ less for the next character you play this turn.

Flavor text: Lanterns fill the sky on one special night, beacons of hope and love.

## Official rulings

- Q: Say I have Stitch – Rock Star in play and a cost 3 character in my hand. If I use Lantern so I can pay 1 {I} less to play that character, can I then exert the character to draw a card?
  A: No. It’s not the character’s cost that’s reduced by Lantern but rather the amount of ink you need to pay in order to play it. The character’s cost would still be 3, so they wouldn’t qualify for the “cost 2 or less” part of the Adoring Fans ability.
