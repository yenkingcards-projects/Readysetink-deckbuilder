Official rulings (1)

If I use Combo Shift to play Dash Parr & Violet Parr – Super Siblings on top of a dry Dash Parr and a drying Violet Parr, what happens? What if the Dash Parr they’re shifting onto is ready but the Violet Parr is exerted?
If you shift on top of a dry character and a drying character, the shifted character will be drying. If you shift them on top of a ready character and an exerted character, they’ll be exerted.
