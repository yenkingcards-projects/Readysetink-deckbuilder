Official rulings (2)

How does Rapunzel’s Act of Kindness ability work?
Act of Kindness is a triggered ability that creates a static replacement effect whenever you choose one of your characters for Support. This static effect lasts until it’s used or until the start of your next turn, whichever comes first. A static replacement effect exists independently of the character it was chosen for, so if another effect causes the character to lose all abilities, the static replacement effect is unaffected.
Official set release notes — Whispers in the Well
If multiple instances of the replacement effect generated by Act of Kindness can apply at the same time, do they stick around if they’re not applied the next time damage is dealt?
No, if an event would be affected by multiple instances of the same replacement effect, their player chooses which one to apply and the other effects cease to exist. For example, if there are two static replacement effects generated from Act of Kindness, they could both apply to the next damage event to that character. The character’s player chooses which one to apply and the other effect ceases to exist. The Black Cauldron The Black Cauldron
