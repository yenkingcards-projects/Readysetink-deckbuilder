## Card text

> HERE COMES THE SMOLDER Whenever this character is challenged, the challenging player chooses and discards a card.

Flavor text: “I didn't want to have to do this, but you leave me no choice....”

## Official rulings

- Q: I have 2 cards in my hand plus a Jafar – Keeper of Secrets in play, and my Jafar challenges Flynn Rider – Charming Rogue. When does Flynn Rider’s ability Here Comes the Smolder happen? How much damage will he take from the challenge, considering Jafar’s Hidden Wonders ability?
  A: Here Comes the Smolder happens as soon as Flynn Rider is chosen to be challenged. This means the challenging player must discard a card before any damage from the challenge is dealt to either character. In this case, you’ll have to discard 1 of your 2 cards, so you’ll have 1 card left. Hidden Wonders increases Jafar’s Strength by an amount equal to the number of cards in your hand, so he’ll have +1 Strength when he deals damage to Flynn Rider in the challenge.
