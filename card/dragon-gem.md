## Card text

> BRING BACK TO LIFE ⟳, 3 ⬡ — Return a character card with Support from your discard to your hand.

Flavor text: Hope shines in even the darkest situations.

## Official rulings

- Q: Can I use a Dragon Gem to return Alice – Growing Girl from my discard to my hand?
  A: No. Even though Alice’s ability Good Advice mentions Support, she herself doesn’t have Support, so she isn’t affected by abilities and effects that interact with it. If you have 2 or more copies of Alice in play, however, they give each other Support. Then abilities and items that interact with Support would apply to all of them.
