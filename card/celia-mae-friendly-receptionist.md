## Card text

> PLEASE HOLD When you play this character, you may pay 1 ⬡ to ready chosen character of yours. They can't quest or challenge for the rest of this turn.

Flavor text: “You'll have to speak up. I can't hear you over my hair.”

## Official rulings

- Q: If I play Celia Mae – Friendly Receptionist and I don’t have any ready ink to use her Please Hold ability, do I still have a character that can’t quest or challenge?
  A: No. If you can’t pay the ability’s ink cost or choose not to pay it, it’s the same as if you choose “no” for the “may” part of the effect. This means that no part of that effect happens, so there’s no character chosen.
