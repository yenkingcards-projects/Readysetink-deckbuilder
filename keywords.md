# Disney Lorcana keyword abilities

Source: https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/

- [Alert](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/alert.md): Lets a character challenge evasive characters as though the Evasive restriction wasn't there. It does not give the character Evasive itself. (rule 8.2)
- [Bodyguard](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/bodyguard.md): The character may enter play already exerted. While a Bodyguard is in play, opposing characters that challenge you must pick a Bodyguard if one is available. (rule 8.3)
- [Boost](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/boost.md): Once during your turn you may pay N ink to put the top card of your deck facedown under this character. Other cards care about how many cards are under a character. (rule 8.4)
- [Challenger](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/challenger.md): While this character is the one challenging, it gets +N Strength. It does nothing when the character is defending. Multiple instances add together. (rule 8.5)
- [Evasive](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/evasive.md): Can't be challenged except by a character that also has Evasive. The most common keyword in the game. (rule 8.6)
- [Reckless](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/reckless.md): The character can't quest, and you can't end your turn while it is ready and able to challenge. It can still be exerted to use abilities or sing songs. A strong but forced attacker. (rule 8.7)
- [Resist](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/resist.md): Reduces damage dealt to this card by N. If that brings the damage to zero, no damage is taken. Multiple instances add together. (rule 8.8)
- [Rush](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/rush.md): The character can challenge the turn it comes into play, skipping the usual drying wait. (rule 8.9)
- [Shift](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/shift.md): Pay the Shift cost instead of the ink cost to play this card on top of one of your characters with the same name. They become one stack. If the card underneath was exerted, the shifted character enters exerted. (rule 8.10)
- [Singer](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/singer.md): This character can exert to sing a song as though its cost were N, even if its real cost is lower. Its actual ink cost doesn't change. (rule 8.11)
- [Sing Together](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/sing-together.md): A song you can play by exerting any number of ready characters whose costs add up to N or more, so several small characters can sing one big song. (rule 8.12)
- [Support](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/support.md): Whenever this character quests, you may add its Strength to another chosen character's Strength for the rest of the turn. (rule 8.13)
- [Vanish](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/vanish.md): If an opponent chooses this character as part of an action's effect, it gets banished afterwards. A built-in risk for anyone who targets it. (rule 8.14)
- [Ward](https://www.readysetink.com/keywords/ward.md): Opponents can't choose this card when resolving an effect. Effects that don't choose, such as ones that hit everything, still affect it. (rule 8.15)
