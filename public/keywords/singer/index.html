Singer (Disney Lorcana keyword)

This character can exert to sing a song as though its cost were N, even if its real cost is lower. Its actual ink cost doesn't change.

Official rule 8.11 (Comprehensive Rules 2.2.0). This keyword appears on 33 cards: 33 Character. By ink: 26 Amber, 7 Ruby, 1 Amethyst, 1 Steel.
