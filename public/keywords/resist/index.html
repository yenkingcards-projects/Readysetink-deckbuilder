Resist (Disney Lorcana keyword)

Reduces damage dealt to this card by N. If that brings the damage to zero, no damage is taken. Multiple instances add together.

Official rule 8.8 (Comprehensive Rules 2.2.0). This keyword appears on 40 cards: 39 Character, 1 Location. By ink: 39 Steel, 2 Ruby, 2 Emerald, 2 Amber, 1 Sapphire, 1 Amethyst.
