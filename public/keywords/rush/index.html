Rush (Disney Lorcana keyword)

The character can challenge the turn it comes into play, skipping the usual drying wait.

Official rule 8.9 (Comprehensive Rules 2.2.0). This keyword appears on 55 cards: 55 Character. By ink: 29 Ruby, 25 Amethyst, 1 Emerald, 1 Sapphire.
