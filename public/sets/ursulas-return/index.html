Ursula's Return: card list

Ursula's Return is Disney Lorcana Set 4, released May 31, 2024. 204 cards first appeared in this set. Reprints of earlier cards are listed under the set they first appeared in.

148 Character, 26 Action, 18 Item, 12 Location. By rarity: 72 Common, 54 Uncommon, 48 Rare, 18 Super Rare, 12 Legendary. By ink: 34 Amber, 34 Amethyst, 34 Emerald, 34 Ruby, 34 Sapphire, 34 Steel.
