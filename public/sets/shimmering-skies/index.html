Shimmering Skies: card list

Shimmering Skies is Disney Lorcana Set 5, released August 23, 2024. 204 cards first appeared in this set. Reprints of earlier cards are listed under the set they first appeared in.

145 Character, 28 Action, 19 Item, 12 Location. By rarity: 72 Common, 54 Uncommon, 48 Rare, 18 Super Rare, 12 Legendary. By ink: 34 Amber, 34 Amethyst, 34 Emerald, 34 Ruby, 34 Sapphire, 34 Steel.
